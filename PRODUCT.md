# RunZone — Product Context (PRODUCT.md)

## 1. Product Overview
RunZone is an AI-powered geospatial fitness and gamified territory-capture SaaS platform. It combines high-performance PostGIS polygon corridor buffering (`ST_Buffer`, `ST_Intersects`, GiST indexing) with Dr. Tim Gabbett's deterministic Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) injury prevention engine, Gemini LLM ZoneCoach briefings, and Strava activity synchronization.

## 2. Core Personas & Audience
- **Competitive Endurance Runners**: Runners training for 5K to marathons who want gamified territory conquest in their city.
- **Injury-Conscious Athletes**: Runners looking to prevent overtraining spikes through scientific ACWR load ratios (Sweet Spot 0.8-1.3).
- **Running Crews & Factions**: Squads competing for geographic control of metropolitan sectors.

## 3. Surface & Mode
- **Mode**: `Operate` (High-density telemetry HUD, real-time map radar, physiological load gauges, activity logs).
- **Voice & Tone**: High-performance, data-driven, tactical, inspiring, scientifically grounded.

## 4. Key Differentiators
1. **Geospatial Territory Buffering**: Converts GPS polylines into 40m territorial polygons via PostGIS.
2. **Deterministic Sports Science**: ACWR gauge indicating acute fatigue spikes vs. chronic capacity.
3. **AI ZoneCoach**: Conversational AI tailored to real-time ACWR and HR vitals.
4. **Strava OAuth2 Sync**: Seamless athlete activity ingestion.
